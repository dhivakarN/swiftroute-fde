# SwiftRoute Logistics — Client Brief

## Client

SwiftRoute Logistics is a mid-size delivery company that manages shipments across multiple cities.

The operations team currently relies on shipment spreadsheets, PDF invoices, and customer-support emails to understand delivery performance.

They want a system that allows the operations manager to ask questions in plain English and receive trustworthy answers backed by the company's data.

## Key Questions

1. Which routes had the highest number of late shipments last week?

2. Why were shipments on those routes late?

3. Which customers or shipments were affected, and what did they pay?

## Success Criteria

- Answers should normally be returned within 5 seconds.
- Numerical answers must be based on shipment data.
- Claims about customer complaints must be traceable to source emails.
- Invoice information must be traceable to the original invoice.
- The system must clearly say when the available data is insufficient to answer a question.
- The system should not invent information that is not present in the source data.
